Feature Guide

Everything ArenaX Offers

A plain-English breakdown of every feature — what it is, how it works, and who benefits most.

⚔️

1v1 Live Coding Duels

Known on the platform as "Iaidō Duels" — named after the samurai art of the instant draw

This is the heart of ArenaX. Two users are matched against each other and given the same algorithmic problem. The first person to submit a correct, working solution wins. It's fast, intense, and completely fair — both players see the same question at the same time. Winners gain ELO rating points; losers lose some. Over time, your ELO reflects your true skill level.

How It Works

1
Click "Find Match"

ArenaX finds you an opponent of similar skill in seconds. If no one is available, a smart bot fills in.

2
Solve the Problem

A coding problem appears. Write your solution in the built-in code editor. Multiple programming languages supported.

3
Win & Climb

The first correct submission wins. ELO is updated instantly. Your tier and rank update in real time.

Example Use Case

Riya, a final-year CS student, uses ArenaX daily to prep for FAANG interviews. She duels five people every morning before college. After two weeks, she's climbed from Bronze (Ashigaru) to Silver (Samurai) — a verifiable skill signal she adds to her resume.

Best for: Software Engineers CS Students Interview Prep
🧠

AI Prompt Battles

Known as "Kotodama Battles" — Kotodama means "word spirit" in Japanese

Instead of writing code, you write instructions (called "prompts") to guide an AI to complete a task. Both competitors get the same challenge, and an AI judge scores whose prompt produced the better result. This is the perfect track for non-programmers who want to master AI tools — because the skill here is clear thinking and communication, not syntax.

How It Works

1
Receive the Challenge

Example: "Write a prompt that gets the AI to create a professional email declining a meeting politely."

2
Write Your Prompt

Craft clear instructions for the AI. Your prompt is sent to GPT-4o and the output is captured.

3
AI Judges the Result

An AI grader scores both outputs on a rubric. The higher-scoring prompt wins the duel.

Example Use Case

Arjun, a consultant at a Big 4 firm, has no coding background but uses ChatGPT daily. ArenaX's Prompt Battles sharpen his skill at getting better AI outputs faster — and his improving ELO rank shows his team he's genuinely skilled at AI, not just guessing.

Best for: Finance & Consulting Product Managers Non-Technical Users

Private Rooms (Dojos)

Challenge a specific friend, classmate, or teammate — no public matchmaking

Private Rooms let you create a custom coding or prompting duel and share a unique code with anyone you want to challenge. Great for college societies that want to run internal tournaments, study groups that practice together, or companies that want to run friendly team challenges.

How It Works

1
Create a Room

Click "Create Private Room" to get a unique 6-digit code.

2
Share the Code

Send it to your friends via WhatsApp, Slack, or any chat. They join using the code.

3
Compete & Repeat

Multiple rounds can happen in the same room. No public leaderboard impact — just fun.

Example Use Case

The CodeCell club at NIT Trichy uses Private Rooms every Friday to run a 30-minute internal "dojo battle" between 20 members. No sign-up fees, no complex setup — just share the code and compete.

Best for: College Clubs Study Groups Team Events

Daily Challenge

One problem. One day. Build an unbreakable streak.

Every day, ArenaX posts a fresh algorithmic problem. Solve it, and your streak counter grows — like a GitHub commit streak or a Duolingo language streak. Your last 90 days of activity are shown as a visual heatmap on your profile. It's a lightweight, low-pressure way to stay sharp every single day.

How It Works

1
Open the Daily Tab

A new problem is waiting every day. Varying difficulty — sometimes easy, sometimes a stretch.

2
Solve It Anytime Today

No timer pressure. Solve it in the morning or at midnight — as long as it's before the day resets.

3
Watch Your Streak Grow

Your heatmap and streak counter update. Share it to show consistent daily practice.

Example Use Case

Meera, a career switcher from banking to tech, commits to solving the Daily Challenge every morning before work. After 60 days, her heatmap is nearly full green — concrete proof of discipline she screenshots for her portfolio and LinkedIn.

Best for: Career Switchers CS Students Freshers

Not ready to duel? Solo Practice gives you random problems to solve whenever you want, with no ranking consequences. Think of it as the training room before entering the arena — a safe space to try new approaches, experiment, and build confidence before going head-to-head with real opponents.

Example Use Case

Kabir is new to competitive coding and doesn't want to lose ELO while still learning. He spends his first week entirely in Solo Practice, trying 10–15 problems, understanding patterns, and only joining live duels once he feels confident enough.

Learn to talk to AI so well that you get dramatically better results — every time

This structured course teaches you how to frame questions, give context, iterate on outputs, and use advanced prompting techniques — from basic queries all the way to complex multi-step instructions. Module 1 is completely free.

Course Structure

1
Module 1 (Free)

Foundations — what prompts are, why they matter, and your first hands-on exercises with AI feedback.

2
Modules 2–4 (Pro)

Intermediate & advanced techniques — chain-of-thought prompting, few-shot examples, role prompting, and iterative refinement.

3
AI Feedback

Every exercise is scored by an AI judge that explains why your prompt worked or didn't — so you improve faster.

Agentic AI Track

Go beyond chatting with AI — learn to build systems that work on your behalf

An "AI Agent" is a program that can take a goal and figure out the steps to achieve it — automatically. This track teaches you how these agents are built, how to think about them, and eventually how to engineer them yourself. It's 4 seasons of learning cards (75 total) ranging from beginner mental models to advanced engineering. Season 0 is free.

The 4 Seasons

S0
Mental Models (Free)

How to think about AI — foundational concepts explained without jargon. Perfect for any background.

S1
Daily Driver (Pro)

Using AI tools effectively in your daily workflow — productivity, automation, and decision support.

S2
4D Operator (Pro)

Advanced reasoning with AI — getting AI to plan, judge, and chain its own outputs intelligently.

S3
Agentic Engineering (Pro)

Build full agentic stacks — AI systems that autonomously execute multi-step workflows.

Example Use Case

Rahul, a founder running a 5-person startup, takes Season 0 over a weekend and immediately rewires how he thinks about AI delegation. By Season 2, he's built a customer feedback summarisation agent that saves his team 4 hours a week.

Best for: Founders Product Managers AI Enthusiasts Engineers

Forge Weekly Challenges

Apply what you learned — submit real work every week, get scored by AI, climb the board

Forge is where learning meets real output. Every week, a new challenge drops across multiple difficulty levels — from foundation exercises to advanced capstone builds. You submit your work (a written answer, a document, code, or a link), and an AI judge scores it automatically against a rubric. Rankings update and ELO changes reflect your performance.

How It Works

1
Challenge Opens

A new Forge challenge appears on Monday. Example prompt: "Describe how you'd use an AI agent to automate expense reports."

2
Submit Your Work

Upload a PDF, paste markdown, submit code, or link a project — whatever the prompt asks for.

3
AI Scores & Ranks

After the deadline, AI judges all submissions and publishes the weekly leaderboard with ELO changes.

Example Use Case

Divya, an MBA student interested in AI strategy, uses Forge weekly challenges to build a portfolio of structured AI thinking. After 8 weeks, she has 8 scored submissions to show recruiters — concrete evidence of applied AI skill, not just theoretical knowledge.

Rank & ELO System

Your skill has a number — and a samurai title to match

Every user starts with an ELO score of 800. Win duels, and your score goes up. Lose, and it drops slightly. The gap between your score and your opponent's determines how many points change hands — beating a stronger opponent earns you more. As your ELO rises, you earn a new samurai tier — each one unlocking a distinct visual badge and a higher place on the global leaderboard.

Referral Program

Invite friends, earn real rewards — including OpenAI API credits

The more people you bring, the better the rewards. The platform tracks every referral milestone automatically.
